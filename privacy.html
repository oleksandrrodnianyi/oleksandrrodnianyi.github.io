Privacy Policy

Reelwick Uploader · Last updated: 12 October 2026

What the app accesses

When the channel owner authorizes it, Reelwick Uploader uses the YouTube Data API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube. With it, the app uploads videos to the owner's channel, edits their metadata and thumbnails, and reads video status and channel information.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to manage the authorizing owner's own channel. The OAuth refresh token is stored locally on the owner's computer in a file only the owner can read. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Other users

The app is not offered to the public and collects no data from anyone other than the authorizing channel owner.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from using the token.

Reelwick Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
